My app it’s basically a music player application with playlists of music and sound types 
to help people with ADHD concentrate, without being to distracting or mundane. 
Our solution consist of two screens, as we can see in the image
below, one screen is the starting screen which is the menu user uses to choose a playlist of a
particular genre of music and the second screen is the playlist itself.
